App Installation & Troubleshooting

Installing the Direct Android APK

  • Download the official APK from our Home page.
  • Open the downloaded naam-jap-v1.2.0.apk file from your browser downloads or Android Files app.
  • If prompted on Android, enable “Install unknown apps” for your browser or file manager in Android Settings for this installation.
  • When our Google Play Store listing becomes live, updates and installs will also be handled directly via Google Play.

Daily Reminder Notifications Not Appearing

  • Open Android Settings → Apps → Naam Jap → Notifications and verify notifications are allowed (required on Android 13+).
  • Ensure your reminder toggle and scheduled time are turned on inside Naam Jap’s Reminders settings.
  • Some phone manufacturers apply aggressive battery optimization that can delay alarms; setting Naam Jap battery usage to Unrestricted can help scheduled local reminders fire on time.

Chant Sound or Vibration Not Working

  • Check the sound and haptic toggles inside Naam Jap Settings.
  • Ensure your phone is not muted or in Silent/Do Not Disturb mode, and note that instant chant voice playback is bundled for the 5 built-in mantras (Radha, Ram, Krishna, Shiv, Waheguru), while custom user mantras use tap counting and haptic feedback.

Resetting Your Local Data

To clear completed session history or reset all locally saved counters and mantras cleanly, go to Settings → Manage Your Data inside the app.

How to Report a Technical Problem

When writing to pinakmahadev@gmail.com, including a few details helps us reproduce and resolve the issue much faster:

  • Your device model (for example, Pixel, Samsung Galaxy, Redmi, OnePlus).
  • Your Android version (for example, Android 13, 14, or 15).
  • The screen or feature where the issue occurred (Home, 3D Mala, Sadhana, Settings, Reminders, or APK install).
  • Short step-by-step description of what happened and a screenshot if comfortable sharing one.

Privacy & Data Inquiries

Naam Jap does not create user accounts or store chanting data on developer servers. For full details on local device storage, Google AdMob, Google UMP consent controls, and Firebase SDKs, please read our Privacy Policy or email pinakmahadev@gmail.com.
