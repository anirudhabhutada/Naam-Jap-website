Mantra Counter App for Focused Daily Chanting

Naam Jap is an Android mantra counting application built for quiet, distraction-free repetition—whether you recite a built-in sacred name or add your own personal mantra.

Built-In Mantras & Custom Mantra Creation

Inside the app’s Select Mantra sheet, you can start immediately with five pre-configured mantras—Ram Ram, Om Namah Shivaya, Jai Shri Ram, Radhe Radhe, and Narayan—each paired with optional bundled recitation audio, or tap + Create Mantra to save your own mantra locally on your phone.

Custom mantra text stays strictly on your device in local app storage and is never uploaded to external analytics or user accounts.

How Naam Jap Works as a Digital Mantra Counter

  • Preset & Custom Targets: Pick quick mala counts of 11, 21, 51, 108, 216, 501, or 1008, or enter any numeric target that fits your daily vow.
  • Instant Tap & Haptic Confirmation: Every tap on the chanting screen increments your count with optional tactile vibration so you do not have to stare at the numbers while chanting.
  • Ad-Free Active Chanting Screen: While Naam Jap is free and supported by Google AdMob on non-chanting screens, no banner or pop-up ads appear during active mantra counting.
  • 14 App Languages: Use the interface in English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese, Sanskrit, or Urdu.
