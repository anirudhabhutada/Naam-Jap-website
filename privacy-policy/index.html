Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Naam Jap mobile application handles information on your device and through third-party Google AdMob and Firebase services.

App Name: Naam Jap Package ID: com.naamjap.app.naam_jap Effective Date: November 12, 2026 Last Updated: November 12, 2026

1. Developer Information & Overview

Naam Jap (“the App”) is a devotional digital Rudraksha Mala and mantra counting application developed and published for Android (com.naamjap.app.naam_jap) and supported by this official website (https://naamjap.app/).

You do not need to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, or phone number to use Naam Jap. Your chanting counts, custom mantras, targets, and reminder schedules are stored locally on your device. To offer the app free of charge and maintain app reliability, Naam Jap integrates Google AdMob (with Google User Messaging Platform consent management) and Google Firebase services (Analytics, Crashlytics, and Remote Config).

For any privacy questions, contact the developer at pinakmahadev@gmail.com.

2. Quick Summary: Local Storage vs. External Services

Data Category Where It Is Processed Purpose
Custom mantras, Jap counts, completed malas, streaks, targets, theme, language, sound & haptic settings Stored locally on your device only (app-private preferences) Core app functionality and offline chanting progress
Daily reminder times & schedule preferences Stored and scheduled locally on your device via OS notifications Local daily Sadhana reminders (no push server or account required)
Advertising ID (AAID), IP address (coarse region), ad interaction data Sent via HTTPS to Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) & UMP SDK Serving and measuring ads and respecting regional consent choices

3. Information Stored Only on Your Device

Naam Jap does not operate user accounts or a developer-hosted user database. The following preferences and Sadhana records are written solely to your device’s app-private local storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Selected interface language (from 14 supported languages) and theme preference (Light, Dark, or System).
  • Active mantra selection and any custom mantras you type into the app.
  • Target count settings, active session count, completed session history, total Jap count, and daily streak records.
  • Audio feedback and haptic vibration preferences.
  • Daily reminder schedules configured using on-device local notifications and your device’s local timezone.
  • Local ad cooldown timestamps and version-update dismissal timestamps.

The developer does not collect, read, or upload your personal custom mantra text, religious selections, or chanting history to any developer server.

In addition, the interactive 3D Rudraksha Mala view runs via a local loopback bridge (127.0.0.1) to load bundled offline 3D assets directly inside the app. It does not load external websites, store web cookies, or track browsing activity.

4. Information Sent to External Services

While the developer does not collect your personal contact information, the third-party Google SDKs compiled into Naam Jap automatically communicate with Google and Firebase servers over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections when internet access is available.

A. Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Naam Jap uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (google_mobile_ads) to display advertisements that support free access to the app:

  • Ad Placements: Native advertisements appear on non-chanting screens, interstitial advertisements may appear after a completed session (subject to a cooldown timer and first-session protection), and rewarded video ads are strictly opt-in from Settings when unlocking the temporary Golden Divine theme.
  • Ad-Free Active Chanting Screen: No advertisements are displayed on the active 3D Rudraksha Mala chanting screen.
  • Information Processed by AdMob: Depending on your consent choices and Android settings, Google Mobile Ads may process your Android Advertising ID (AD_ID permission), IP address (used for approximate geographic region and fraud prevention), device model, OS version, and ad impression/click events.
  • Consent Management (UMP): Before loading ads, Naam Jap invokes Google’s User Messaging Platform (ConsentManager). In applicable regions (such as the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and relevant U.S. states), you are presented with a consent dialog to choose personalized or non-personalized ads, and you can revisit those preferences anytime under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices when available for your region.

B. Google Firebase Analytics

Naam Jap uses Firebase Analytics to record aggregated engagement events such as app opens, session starts/completions, milestone counts, theme or language changes, and whether a custom or built-in mantra type was used. In code, custom mantra text is never logged to Firebase Analytics; only an indicator (custom or default) is attached. Firebase Analytics processes app-instance identifiers, basic device/OS information, and coarse IP-derived location.

C. Google Firebase Crashlytics

Naam Jap uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix application crashes and unexpected errors. When a crash or logged non-fatal error occurs, Crashlytics collects stack traces, device state, OS version, Crashlytics installation UUID, and two non-personal diagnostic keys: selected app language and active screen name.

D. Google Firebase Remote Config

Naam Jap uses Firebase Remote Config solely to check whether a newer or required app update is available (minimum_app_version, latest_app_version, force_update). Remote Config uses Firebase installation metadata without receiving your personal Sadhana data.

5. Android App Permissions

  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Used by Google Mobile Ads SDK on Android 13+ in accordance with user consent and system privacy settings.
  • Network Access (INTERNET): Used to load Google AdMob ads, Google UMP consent forms, Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics/Remote Config requests, and downloadable typography assets when needed.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used solely for optional tactile haptic feedback when counting beads on the Rudraksha Mala.
  • Notifications & Boot Receiver (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used solely when you enable local Daily Sadhana Reminders so your scheduled reminders persist after a phone restart.

Naam Jap does not request location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, call log, or external storage file permissions.

6. Why Information Is Used

Information processed locally or via Google SDKs is used only to:

  • Provide reliable mantra counting, 3D mala visuals, Sadhana history, themes, audio/haptic controls, and local reminders.
  • Support free availability of the app through Google AdMob advertising while respecting user consent rules.
  • Monitor app stability, investigate crashes, check for required updates, and understand general feature usage.

The developer does not sell your personal information and does not build personal user profiles across unrelated apps.

8. Data Security Practices

Network communications between the integrated Google Mobile Ads and Firebase SDKs and Google’s servers are transmitted using HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit. Local Sadhana data stored by Naam Jap resides inside Android’s application-private storage sandbox. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% immune to risk, so users should keep their device operating system updated and secure.

9. Data Retention, Account Deletion & Your Deletion Controls

No User Accounts: Naam Jap does not support user registration or cloud accounts, so there is no remote user account to delete.

Deleting Local App Data: Your local Sadhana history, custom mantras, targets, and settings stay on your device until you choose to remove them. You can erase completed session history or reset all local app data anytime inside the app via Settings → Manage Your Data, or by clearing the app’s storage in Android System Settings / uninstalling Naam Jap.

Third-Party SDK Data: Diagnostic logs, analytics data, and ad-related identifiers processed by Google AdMob and Firebase are retained according to Google and Firebase platform retention schedules.

10. Your Privacy Choices & Controls

  • Ad Consent Controls: Where supported by Google UMP (EEA/UK/Switzerland and applicable U.S. states), you can update consent choices inside the app under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Device Advertising Controls: On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID or limit personalized ads in Android Settings → Privacy / Google → Ads.
  • Notifications: You can enable, pause, or revoke reminder notifications anytime inside Naam Jap Settings or Android Notification Settings.

11. Children’s Privacy

Naam Jap is a general-audience devotional chanting and mantra counter app. It does not require registration and does not knowingly collect personal contact details (such as names, emails, or addresses) from children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and believe personal information was inadvertently submitted through support correspondence or otherwise, please contact us at pinakmahadev@gmail.com so we can assist promptly.
