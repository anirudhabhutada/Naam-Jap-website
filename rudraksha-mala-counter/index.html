Interactive 3D Rudraksha Mala Counter

Built around a bundled 3D Rudraksha Mala model, Naam Jap pairs visual bead progression with gentle tactile vibration and an ad-free chanting screen.

How the 3D Rudraksha Mala Model Works

The 3D viewer bundled inside Naam Jap renders a ring of 54 sculpted Rudraksha counting beads anchored at the bottom by a fixed Guru bead and tassel. As you tap anywhere on the chanting surface, the active golden bead highlight advances smoothly from bead to bead while your numerical session counter tracks toward your chosen target (such as 108—two complete visual rounds around the 54-bead ring—or any custom target).

  • 100% Offline 3D Rendering: The 3D mala assets run locally inside the app without loading external web pages or requiring mobile data during chanting.
  • Haptic & Audio Options: Toggle tactile vibration and instant chant audio directly from the top bar of the Jap screen or in Settings.
  • Light, Dark & Golden Divine Themes: Practice comfortably day or night, or unlock a temporary Golden Divine theme via an optional rewarded ad in Settings.
