Digital Jap Mala: Traditional Practice, Always With You

A physical Rudraksha or Tulsi mala is irreplaceable for seated home sadhana—yet travel, work breaks, and commutes often interrupt routine. A digital Jap Mala bridges that gap.

Respecting Tradition While Supporting Consistency

In traditional bhakti and meditation practice, a mala serves one core purpose: keeping the mind anchored to the mantra instead of tracking arithmetic. Naam Jap brings that same quiet rhythm to your Android smartphone without turning prayer into a noisy social feed.

  • Eyes-Free Tactile Rhythm: Gentle haptic vibration confirms every bead tap so you can focus inward.
  • Daily Thought & Active Naam Home Screen: Begin each session with a calm reflective quote and your active mantra count right on the Home tab.
  • Custom Mala Targets: Practice short 11- or 21-count breaks, standard 108-count rounds, or deeper 216, 501, and 1008 resolutions.
