ABOUT THE APP

A Peaceful Companion for Daily Naam Jap

Designed to help you stay consistent in daily mantra chanting without accounts, logins, or complicated menus.

What Naam Jap Helps You Do

Whether you are keeping a daily 108-bead mala sankalpa or setting a shorter or longer mantra goal during a busy day, Naam Jap gives you a calm digital Rudraksha Mala on your phone.

How the Digital Rudraksha Mala Works

Open the chanting screen and tap anywhere on the interactive 3D Rudraksha Mala (54 sculpted counting beads plus a fixed Guru bead and tassel) to advance smoothly toward your target. Gentle haptic feedback confirms every count, optional instant audio plays built-in mantra recitations, and your active chanting screen stays free of banner or pop-up advertisements.

All counts, completed malas, custom mantras, theme choices, and reminder schedules stay stored locally on your device.

QUESTIONS & SUPPORT

Quick Answers

Key details about how Naam Jap works, advertising, offline use, and privacy.

Does Naam Jap require sign-up or an account?

No. Naam Jap has no login, registration, or user accounts. Your chanting counts, custom mantras, targets, and reminder preferences stay locally on your device.

Does Naam Jap show advertisements?

Yes. Naam Jap is free and supported by Google AdMob advertisements (such as Home screen native ads, occasional interstitial transitions with cooldown limits, and optional rewarded ads to unlock the Golden Divine theme). No ads are shown while you are actively chanting on the mala screen.

Can I customize my mantra and target count?

Yes. You can select any of the 5 built-in mantras or add your own custom mantra text, and set preset counts (11, 21, 51, 108, 216, 501, 1008) or any custom target.

How can I delete my data or contact support?

Because Naam Jap does not create cloud accounts, you can delete your stored data anytime inside the app under Settings → Manage Your Data or by uninstalling the app. For questions, email pinakmahadev@gmail.com.
