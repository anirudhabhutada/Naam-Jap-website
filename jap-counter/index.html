Why Use a Dedicated Digital Jap Counter?

Unlike mechanical tally clickers that reset accidentally or lack session history, the Naam Jap digital Jap counter records your active round, completed malas, lifetime total, and daily streak right inside your phone’s local storage.

  • Automatic Session Saving: Pause at count 38 or 76 and return later without losing your place.
  • Sadhana History & Streaks: View your Total Jap, Completed Malas, Current Streak, Longest Streak, and individual completed sessions with timestamps.
  • Local Daily Reminders: Set morning or evening local Android notifications so your daily Jap habit stays consistent without any cloud push account.
  • Instant Reset & Data Control: Clear session history or reset local counters anytime under Settings → Manage Your Data.
