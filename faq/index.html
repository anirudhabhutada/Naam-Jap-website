Frequently Asked Questions

Everything here reflects the verified features and behavior of the Naam Jap Android app.

What is Naam Jap?

Naam Jap is a devotional Android application designed for daily mantra chanting. It combines an interactive 3D Rudraksha Mala counter, customizable mantra and count targets, optional chant audio and haptic feedback, local reminders, and offline Sadhana progress tracking.

How does the Jap counter and 3D Rudraksha Mala work?

When you start chanting, the 3D Rudraksha Mala appears on the chanting screen. Each tap advances the virtual Rudraksha bead downward, increments your session counter toward your target, plays optional haptic vibration feedback, and saves your progress automatically on your device.

How do I start counting?

Open the app on your Android phone, select or confirm your active mantra and target count on the Home screen, tap to open the chanting screen, and tap anywhere on the mala area to begin counting bead by bead.

Can I customize the mantra?

Yes. Naam Jap includes 5 built-in divine names (Radha, Ram, Krishna, Shiv, and Waheguru) with bundled recitation audio, plus the ability to add your own custom mantra text saved locally on your device.

Can I change the counting target?

Yes. You can quickly pick preset mala targets (11, 21, 51, 108, 216, 501, or 1008) or type your own custom numeric target.

How do daily reminder notifications work?

Daily reminders are scheduled directly on your Android device using local operating-system notifications and your local timezone. They do not require creating an account or connecting to a push notification server. You can turn reminders on or off anytime inside the app or Android system Settings.

How do I manage sound and vibration (haptics)?

Open Settings inside Naam Jap to toggle haptic feedback and chant audio feedback. Built-in voice audio applies to the 5 pre-included mantras; custom mantras count cleanly with visual feedback and optional vibration.

How do I change the app language?

Inside the Android app, open Settings → Language to switch between 14 supported languages: English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese, Sanskrit, and Urdu.

Why does the application show ads, and are ads shown while chanting?

Naam Jap is free to download and uses Google AdMob advertisements (such as native ads on non-chanting screens, interstitial transitions with cooldown limits after meaningful sessions, and optional rewarded ads in Settings to try the Golden Divine theme) to keep the app free. Active chanting on the 3D Rudraksha Mala screen stays free of ads so your focus is uninterrupted.

Does Naam Jap require sign-up or an internet connection?

No user account or sign-up is needed, and core chanting, bead counting, custom mantras, themes, and local Sadhana history work offline. An internet connection is only used for Google AdMob ads, UMP consent prompts, Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics diagnostics, and version-update checks.

How can I clear my history or delete my data?

Because no remote user accounts exist, all personal chanting history and custom mantras live only on your device. Open Settings → Manage Your Data inside the app to clear completed session history or reset all local app data, or uninstall the app.

How can I report a technical problem or read the Privacy Policy?

Visit our Support & Contact page or email pinakmahadev@gmail.com with your Android device model, OS version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Full data disclosures are published in our Privacy Policy.
