A Beginner’s Guide to Ram Naam Jap

By Naam Jap Editorial Team • Updated November 26, 2026

Ram Naam Jap is cherished across India for its simplicity—a short, heartfelt repetition that anyone can practice daily without elaborate preparation.

Why Devotees Love Ram Naam Jap

Because “Ram Ram” or “Jai Shri Ram” is concise and rhythmic, it naturally pairs with steady breathing and calm attention. Both Ram Ram and Jai Shri Ram are included out of the box in the Naam Jap Android app with optional bundled chant audio.

Three Simple Steps to Stay Consistent

  1. Start With One Target: Pick 108 counts (or 51 if you are short on time) and stick with it for 7 days.
  2. Silence Distractions: Switch on haptic vibration and keep your focus on the rhythm of the name rather than rushing through numbers.
  3. Use Daily Reminders: Set a gentle morning or evening local reminder inside Naam Jap Settings so your streak builds naturally.
