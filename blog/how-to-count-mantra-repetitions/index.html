How to Count Mantra Repetitions Accurately

By Naam Jap Editorial Team • Updated November 26, 2026

Counting mentally while reciting a mantra quickly splits your concentration. Using a reliable physical or digital counter keeps your mind free for devotion.

1. Finger-Segment Counting (Karamala)

In traditional karamala, the thumb moves across the natural phalanges (segments) of the fingers on the right hand. It requires no equipment, though keeping track of multiple 108-count rounds mentally can still feel tricky during longer sessions.

2. Traditional 108-Bead Mala

Holding a 108-bead Rudraksha or Tulsi mala lets you advance one bead per repetition without crossing the central Guru bead when reversing direction for a second round.

3. Digital Mantra Counter App

When you are away from home or want automatic daily totals, a mantra counter app like Naam Jap lets you tap anywhere on the screen with gentle haptic vibration, auto-saves your progress if interrupted, and records completed rounds in local session history.
