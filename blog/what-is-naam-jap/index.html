What Is Naam Jap? A Beginner’s Guide

By Naam Jap Editorial Team • Updated November 26, 2026

Naam Jap (also spelled Naam Japa) is the devotional practice of calmly repeating a sacred name or short mantra with steady attention.

Meaning of “Naam” and “Jap”

In Indian spiritual traditions, Naam refers to a divine name (such as Ram, Radhe, Shiv, or Narayan), and Jap (or Japa) means quiet, deliberate repetition. Rather than rushing through a checklist, the goal of Naam Jap is to bring scattered thoughts back to one peaceful anchor.

Practitioners may recite aloud (vaikhari), softly in a whisper (upanshu), or silently in the mind (manasik).

How Beginners Can Start a Daily Routine

  • Pick One Familiar Mantra: Start with a name or short mantra you already feel connected to, such as Ram Ram, Om Namah Shivaya, Jai Shri Ram, Radhe Radhe, or Narayan.
  • Choose a Realistic Target: You do not have to begin with many malas on day one. Starting with 11, 21, 51, or a single 108 round builds consistency without pressure.
  • Keep a Fixed Time: Pairing Jap with morning quiet time or an evening pause makes it easier to sustain day after day.
  • Use a Mala or Counter Only as Support: Whether you hold a physical mala or use a digital Jap counter, the counter handles arithmetic so your attention stays on the chant.
